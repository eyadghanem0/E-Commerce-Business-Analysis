## Introduction
This dashboard analyzes the performance of an E-Commerce business. Many KPIs were utilized to conclude various insights about marketing, products and sales.
## About The Dataset
The dataset features synthetic data for an E-Commerce business. It has four original tables: Sales, Marketing, Products and Customers. These tables are well organized and contain high-quality,  connected data, and are suitable for insight discovery and visualization.
## Brief Discussion
- Overview Page: Product categories and information about the customers were presented. Sales KPIs indicate that the business is successful and profitable.
- Products Page: Electronics clearly outperform all other categories in revenue and profit. However, other categories may have higher gross margin (but a lower PPU) and the difference in quantity sold isn't considerably big. So, if sold in large quantities, these categories will generate more profit. Finally, sales increased then declined again until the end of 2024.
- Marketing Page: Marketing profit is not significantly high, but it served a great purpose by attracting customers since customer acquisition cost(CAC) is way lower than profit per customer(PPC). In addition, marketing succeeded in bringing customers, as the number of joined customers remained consistent without an increase in marketing spend, and all existing customers returned in 2024.
- Sales Page: Top 10 customers for the selected period are shown. Despite Whatsapp being ranked the second in marketing profit, both FB and IG outranked it in sales, while the Website stayed on the lead. Also, discounts around 10% are the most suitable to keep decent profit as well as maintaining a large number of buyers. Finally, orders by day of the week can help the business to focus on certain days and organize off-days for employees.
